Earbud & Headphone Gear: what actually matters

Most complaints about earbuds — falling out, muffled sound, short battery life — trace back to fit and case condition, not the earbuds themselves. Here's what to check before assuming you need a new pair.

Ear tip fit

The wrong tip size is the single most common cause of poor sound and earbuds falling out — most earbuds ship with 3 sizes, and few people try all of them.

Case condition

A worn charging case with loose contacts is a common, overlooked cause of "my earbuds won't charge" — often cheaper to fix with a replacement case than new earbuds.

Bluetooth compatibility

Older devices without Bluetooth may need an adapter — check the transmitter's Bluetooth version matches or exceeds your earbuds' minimum requirement.

Ear tip sizing fixes more than you'd expect

A loose seal from an ill-fitting tip is the most common reason earbuds sound thin or fall out during movement — not a defect in the earbuds. Most earbuds ship with small, medium, and large silicone tips, but people default to whatever's pre-installed. Trying all three, and considering foam tips for a firmer seal, solves a surprising number of "these earbuds are bad" complaints before you spend money on a new pair.

The charging case matters more than people think

Charging contacts inside the case wear down with pocket lint and repeated use, and a case that no longer makes clean contact will show up as "earbuds not charging" even when the earbuds themselves are fine. Replacement cases are available for many popular models and cost far less than new earbuds — worth checking before assuming the whole set needs replacing.

Bluetooth adapters for older devices

If you're trying to use wireless earbuds with an older TV, gym equipment, or an in-flight entertainment system without Bluetooth, a small Bluetooth transmitter plugged into the audio jack or USB port can bridge the gap. Check the transmitter's supported Bluetooth version against your earbuds' minimum requirement — mismatches show up as connection drops or noticeable audio lag, especially for video.
